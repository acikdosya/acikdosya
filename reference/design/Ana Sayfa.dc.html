How we work

Three confidence levels

Every value carries one of three states. The state does not say the value is correct — it says what kind of source it came from. The same three states look identical in tables, map legends, 3D labels and the timeline.

official

Manufacturer publication, institutional statement or procurement document. A link is given; if the link dies, an archived copy and its date replace it.

press

Secondary source: news report, magazine, trade show or conference note. Unverified, but on record and not hidden.

estimate

Açık Dosya calculation. Method, inputs and margin of error are printed under every estimate; you can dispute it and send a correction.

Conflict

When a value has more than one source, all of them are listed. A conflict is not an error — it is part of the record.
